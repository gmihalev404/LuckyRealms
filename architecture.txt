Adding a new game requires:

1. A new GAME_CONFIGS entry
   - symbols
   - payouts
   - bonus
   - theme metadata
   - statistics key
   - music
   - SFX configuration

2. New theme styles
   - home card styles
   - slot page styles

3. Background audio asset

No changes to slots.js, sound.js, home.js or slots.html are required.
